# Closed beta (target: about 10 people)

**Set up (5 min):** Admin → *Beta & legal* → sign-up mode **Invite only**; fill the legal contact; create one code per person (1 use, 30 days). Optional: *Starts on plan* = Pro so testers try paid limits without paying. Send each person their code plus the sign-up link.

**Who:** 3–4 people who model things professionally (agronomy, finance, research), 3–4 curious non-experts, 2–3 skeptics. Not friends who will only be polite.

**Ask them to do (30–45 min):**
1. Paste an idea from their own field **without** equations; open the model; change the sliders.
2. Paste an equation or a CSV; compare what they get.
3. Save a second idea; look at Knowledge Universe; accept or reject a suggested link.
4. Press *Send feedback* once: what confused you, what would you pay for?

**What to measure (Admin → Finance / Users):** analyses per user, failed analyses (AI output rejected by the engine), average cost per analysis, who hit the free quota, who asked for more. **Read the raw feedback** — the first 10 conversations teach more than any chart.

**Decisions this beta should make:** (a) is the free quota (10/month) too tight or too generous? (b) which model tier is good enough (see `docs/EVAL.md`)? (c) would anyone pay $12/month, and for what — more AI analyses, cloud sync, exports? (d) what do people try that the app cannot do?

**Safety:** keep a monthly spend limit in the AI provider consoles; keep the daily per-user cap on; tell testers the data may be reset and that results are hypotheses.

**After:** switch to *Open* (or keep invite-only and grow by waves), enable PayPal live billing (`docs/BILLING-PAYPAL.md`), and publish the final Terms/Privacy after legal review.
